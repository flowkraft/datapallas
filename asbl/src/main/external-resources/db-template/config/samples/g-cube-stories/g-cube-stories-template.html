Cube Stories

One card per cube — tick a field, see the answer, see the SQL

Every cube DataPallas ships, live. Each card below is one cube of the semantic layer, with the questions it was written to answer. Click Show Me on a question and the fields it needs are ticked for you; tick your own and the answer follows. Each card also offers Show Config, the cube’s own definition, and Show SQL, the statement your selection would be answered by — for any of the databases DataPallas writes SQL for, not only the one the rows come from. Pick it once, top right, and every card on the page is written for it. Read more →

Customer Support 1 cube

Customer Support

Support Desk

Grain: One row per ticket (cube_demo.support_tickets)

Who sees which rows: an agent sees their own team’s tickets, and the desk finds the team from the email they signed in with; the support-managers group, an admin and a report author see the whole desk. A share link has nobody behind it and sees none of them, unless it was made with the attribute desk = all, which lets it read the whole desk, as this page’s own link does. It is the cube’s own rule, written in its SQL, and it is on every answer this card gives — totals and drill included.
